## 1. Principles (what every sound must obey)

1. **The hollow is the instrument.** Water is the default bed (strongest evidence for restoration, 06 §1.1); birds are sparse accents; music sits inside nature, not on top of it. Music and nature are balanced within about ±1 LU (§4.6).
2. **Low arousal through tempo, mode and space** (06 §1.2): a slow harmonic rhythm (one chord per ~27 s), a bright-but-calm mode (Lydian), lots of silence (motif density 0.25–0.55, ≥ 4 s between onsets, a 45–90 s music rest every 4–7 min). We never claim the music "slows your heart" (unsupported, 06 §1.2).
3. **Consonance by construction.** Melody and SFX pitches come only from the current chord's 5-note set, which has no semitone pairs (Plomp & Levelt 1965), so any order of notes is consonant. Three pitch classes — **D, G and A** — are in every set (`harmony.universal_pcs`), so fixed-pitch UI sounds use only those.
4. **Soft edges.** Raised-cosine attacks of 1–10 ms, decays matched to the gesture (06 §3), everything low-passed ≤ 6 kHz, the warmth carried by 200–600 Hz partials because phone speakers have no bass. Pads have a high shelf at 2.5 kHz; nothing has energy above 10 kHz.
5. **Good news only** (E2, A3). There is no error buzz: "not possible" is a soft wooden *tok* a fourth lower. No thunder, ever (GDD §6.2). No stingers; changes take 30–90 s.
6. **Synchronised and complete when muted** (I1, A4). Sound, haptic and visual fire in the same frame on touch-down; every meaningful sound has a visual twin (§6) and every reward has a sound (S02, S04, S09, S10, S16).
7. **Never the same twice in a row.** Variants are never repeated back to back, chord successions never repeat, voices run on incommensurate periods (23.5 / 25.875 / 29.9375 s, Eno 1996) against 27.4 s pads and 58.3 s beds, so the texture never re-aligns.

## 2. Bus layout (C15, C17, D10; verified)

`res://default_bus_layout.tres` is the engine's default `audio/buses/default_bus_layout` path, so it loads with no project setting. It was **written by Godot itself** (`tools/audio/make_bus_layout.gd` builds the graph from `manifest.buses` and calls `ResourceSaver.save(AudioServer.generate_bus_layout(), …)`), then reloaded and read back property by property [V].

| # | Bus | Gain | Send | Effects (in order) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Master | 0 dB | — | `AudioEffectStereoEnhance` `pan_pullout=0.0`, **disabled** (the Mono setting enables it) → `AudioEffectHardLimiter` `ceiling_db=-1.5` | `pan_pullout=0` folds to (L+R)/2 in both channels (`servers/audio/effects/audio_effect_stereo_enhance.cpp:37-49`) [V]. The HardLimiter is a sample-peak look-ahead limiter; −1.5 leaves 0.5 dB for inter-sample peaks under the −1 dBTP goal. `AudioEffectLimiter` is deprecated (`doc/classes/AudioEffectLimiter.xml:2`). |
| 1 | Music | −3 dB | Master | `AudioEffectLowPassFilter` `cutoff_hz=20000`, `resonance=0.5`, `db=0` (one 12 dB/oct biquad) | Tone duck and night darkening. **Never set resonance to 0**: Godot maps it straight to biquad Q and clamps Q ≤ 0 to 0.0001, which attenuates the whole bus by ≈ 40 dB (`servers/audio/audio_filter_sw.cpp:68-71`) [V]. |
| 2 | Ambience | −6 dB | Master | none | Space is baked into the beds. |
| 3 | SFX | 0 dB | Master | `AudioEffectReverb` `room_size=0.5`, `damping=0.25`, `spread=0.8`, `hipass=0.15`, `predelay_msec=20`, `predelay_feedback=0`, `dry=1`, `wet=0.15` | One small warm room for world sounds (C17). `damping` is *reflectivity* (higher = brighter; in-loop LP = ((d/2+0.5)²)·10 kHz = 3.9 kHz here). With `predelay_feedback=0` the predelay stage passes audio through untouched (`reverb_filter.cpp:61-85`) [V]. RT60 ≈ 1.07 s (measured on our port). |
| 4 | UI | −3 dB | Master | none | Dry and immediate. |

Engine notes for the Tech Lead:
- Buses sleep after 2 s below −60 dB (`audio/buses/channel_disable_*` defaults, `audio_server.cpp:1527`): keep the defaults.
- The SFX reverb was ported to numpy line by line (`tools/audio/dsp.py:godot_reverb`) and checked against a MovieWriter capture of Godot's own SFX bus fed with an impulse: wet energy within **0.03 dB**, residual at the 16-bit capture floor [V]. That port bakes the Web twins (§9).
- Effect parameters can be tweened at runtime: `AudioEffectFilterInstance::process` re-reads `cutoff`/`resonance` every mix block (`servers/audio/effects/audio_effect_filter.cpp:71-77`) [V].

## 3. Loudness and spectral contracts (A1, A2)

### 3.1 Metering

BS.1770-4 / EBU R128, implemented in `tools/audio/loudness.py` (K-weighting = +4 dB shelf at 1.5 kHz, Q 0.7071, then HP 38 Hz, Q 0.5; 400 ms blocks, −70 / −10 LU gates; short-term 3 s; LRA per EBU 3342; true peak at 4× oversampling) and **cross-checked against pyloudnorm 0.2.0 on every long file (gate: ≤ 0.05 LU apart; observed ≤ 0.013)**. Mono is measured as dual-mono because Godot copies mono to both channels (`scene/resources/audio_stream_wav.cpp:206-211`) [V].

### 3.2 Contract

| Scope | Metric | Target | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole mix, ≥ 10 min idle (A1) | LUFS-I | −18 | ±1 LU in `synth.py` (simulated mix); CI on a real capture fails beyond ±2 (C19) |
| | true peak / short-term / LRA | — | ≤ −1 dBTP (before the limiter) / ≤ −14 LUFS-S / ≤ 8 LU |
| Beds and pads (each file) | LUFS-I | −18.0 | ±1 LU after encoding; ≤ −1.5 dBTP before encoding and ≤ −1.0 after |
| SFX and notes (each file) | LUFS-M (loudest 400 ms) | bank target (§6, §4.4) | ±1 LU; sample peak ≤ −6 dBFS; TP ≤ −5 dBTP |
| Cues (as heard at Master) | LUFS-M | §6 table | every reachable file within ±1.5 LU |
| Spectrum (A2), every asset | energy > 10 kHz | — | ≤ 0.5 % |
| | energy 2–5 kHz | — | ≤ 5 % *tonal* (instruments, pads, UI), *foley* (paper, leaves, soil) and *air* (wind); ≤ 20 % *water*; *accent* (birds, crickets) unconstrained but … |
| Accents in the mix | heard level | — | … ≥ 15 dB under the beds (birds: LUFS-M max vs beds LUFS-I; crickets: LUFS-I vs beds) |
| Bright noise (air, water beds) | 1–16 Hz AM index of the 2.5–5.5 kHz envelope (Kumar et al. 2008) | — | ≤ 0.20 whenever ≥ 1 % of the energy is in that band (steady noise reads ≈ 0.04; rain reads 0.105) |
| Loops | seam curvature (2nd difference across the wrap ÷ p99.9 inside the file) | ≤ 1 | ≤ 1.0 float, ≤ 2.0 as Godot plays it; plus the Vorbis loop-fade gate (§10.3) |

"Heard" means file level + layer gain + bus gain. Bank file targets are chosen so that most layer gains are 0 dB (Appendix C).

## 4. Music system

### 4.1 Key, tempo, grid (D10, C18)

- **F Lydian** (F G A B C D E): major-family valence with the raised 4th (B over F) as the signature colour, and no dominant pull.
- **70 BPM, 4/4.** One beat is 44100·60/70 = **37 800 samples** exactly; a bar is 3.4286 s; an 8-bar pad clip is **1 209 600 samples** = 27.428571 s = 5376 blocks of 225 samples, so the block-modulated filters stay periodic (C18) [V: every pad file's length is gated].
- Tempo only quantises chord changes (`TRANSITION_FROM_TIME_NEXT_BAR`) and gives the motif rhythms (eighth 0.4286 s, dotted eighth 0.6429 s, quarter 0.8571 s). Nothing pulses: no drums, no ostinato.

### 4.2 Chords, voicings and pitch sets

Sets are 06 §2.2 unchanged. Voicings were re-chosen by computing the Sethares (1993) roughness of the low-passed saw partials for each candidate (lower = smoother) — 06's Dm9 voicing had an E4–F4 semitone at 330/349 Hz (a 20 Hz beat, right at maximum roughness) and scored 1.16.

| Clip | Chord | Day voicing (MIDI) | Night voicing | Melody / SFX set | Root · fifth | Roughness day (06's) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Fmaj9` | Fmaj9 | F3 C4 E4 G4 A4 (53 60 64 67 69) | F3 C4 E4 G4 | F G A C D | F · C | 0.84 (1.03) |
| `GoverF` | G/F — the Lydian II over I | F3 B3 D4 G4 A4 (53 59 62 67 69) | F3 D4 G4 B4 | G A B D E | G · D | 0.91 (same) |
| `Dm9` | Dm9 (Fmaj7 over D) | D3 F4 A4 C5 E5 (50 65 69 72 76) | D3 A3 F4 C5 | D F G A C | D · A | 0.48 (1.16) |
| `Am7` | Am7(add11) | A3 E4 G4 C5 D5 (57 64 67 72 74) | A3 E4 G4 C5 | A C D E G | A · E | 0.57 (0.54) |
| `Cmaj7` | Cmaj7 | G3 C4 E4 G4 B4 (55 60 64 67 71) | G3 C4 E4 B4 | C D E G A | C · G | 0.63 (same) |

Night voicings drop a voice and open up (roughness 0.34–0.54). The successor chord is chosen uniformly from the other four (no immediate repeat).

### 4.3 L1 pads (`AudioStreamInteractive`)

**Recipe** (`tools/audio/music.py`, R-B of 06 §4.3 extended): per note 4 PolyBLEP saws per channel (−9/−3/+4/+10 cents ± 1 cent jitter) plus a sine body; a slow per-voice swell (1–3 cycles per clip); a 24 dB/oct low-pass whose cutoff breathes ±0.4 octave (2 cycles per clip by day, 1 by night, opposite phase per channel); a quiet sub-octave sine for headphones; a faint octave "air" on the top note by day; a high shelf at 2.5 kHz (−4.5 dB day, −6 dB night); then a 3.2–3.6 s dark synthetic hall at 30–36 % wet, applied by **circular convolution** so the reverb tail of the clip's end is already in its start.

| Set | Cutoff centre | LFO | Reverb | Used by profiles | Centroid (measured) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| day (`pad_day_*`) | 1.3 kHz | 2 cycles / clip | RT60 3.2 s, 30 % | dawn, day (incl. golden hour), dusk | 650–720 Hz |
| night (`pad_night_*`) | 620 Hz | 1 cycle / clip | RT60 3.6 s, 36 % | night | 350–490 Hz |

**Stream construction** (verified headless in 4.7.2: a switch requested at t = 1.31 s landed on the first bar line at t = 3.47 s and `get_current_clip_index()` went 0 → 1):

```gdscript
var pads := AudioStreamInteractive.new()
pads.clip_count = 10                       # clip i = manifest harmony.pad_clip_order[i] (5 day clips, then 5 night)
pads.set_clip_name(i, &"pad_day_fmaj9")    # clip name == asset id
pads.set_clip_stream(i, load(path))        # AudioStreamOggVorbis, imported loop=true bpm=70 beat_count=32 bar_beats=4
pads.add_transition(AudioStreamInteractive.CLIP_ANY, AudioStreamInteractive.CLIP_ANY,
	AudioStreamInteractive.TRANSITION_FROM_TIME_NEXT_BAR, AudioStreamInteractive.TRANSITION_TO_TIME_START,
	AudioStreamInteractive.FADE_CROSS, 6.0)   # 6 beats = 5.14 s linear crossfade
```

The crossfade is linear in amplitude (`modules/interactive_music/audio_stream_interactive.cpp:900-960`): about −3 dB of power in the middle for uncorrelated pads. That is intended — it reads as the room breathing between chords.

### 4.4 L2 generative voices (`AudioStreamPolyphonic`)

| Voice | Bank | Period | Notes per motif | Rendered range (every F-Lydian note) | File LUFS-M |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| e-piano | `ep` (R-A1: 2-op FM 1:1, index 1.35 → 0.2 in ~0.3 s, warm sine body, soft tine kept < 3.2 kHz, baked 1.5 s room at 20 %) | 23.5 s | 2 | F3–B5 (18) | −20, −1.5 dB/octave above C5 |
| marimba | `mar` (R-A2: felt-mallet bar 1 : 3.93 : 9.2, resonator-long fundamental) | 25.875 s | 1 | F3–E5 (14) | −21 (tilted) |
| chime | `chime` (R-F: free–free bar modes 1 : 2.7565 : 5.4039 : 8.9330 with pairs detuned 0.4–1.4 Hz so they shimmer instead of roughening) | 29.9375 s | 3 | C5–B6 (14) | −22 (tilted) |

Each voice has its own timer from boot. On a tick, a motif starts only if music is on, it is not a rest or a hush, `randf() ≤ density` and ≥ 4 s have passed since the last onset of any voice. The motif:
1. `m = snap(base + randi_range(-3, 3))` in the current chord's set, octave-wrapped into the bank range;
2. each next note `m = pent_step(m, pick([+1, +1, +2, -1, -2]))` (steps through the 5-note set), wrapped into range;
3. inter-onset `pick([0.4286, 0.6429, 0.8571], weights [0.4, 0.4, 0.2])` ± 20 ms humanisation;
4. velocity `randf_range(-3, 0)` dB on top of the profile's voice level; `pitch_scale` is **always 1.0** because every note exists as a file (required on Web, §9).

A real excerpt from the simulator (day, clear, seed 7):

```
  6.00 s  chord G/F          47.44 s  ep D5           93.99 s  ep D5
 23.49 s  ep D5              54.00 s  chord Cmaj7     94.85 s  ep E5
 23.93 s  ep G5              70.49 s  ep A4          108.86 s  chord Am7
 30.00 s  chord Am7          71.15 s  ep E4          129.37 s  mar G4
 46.99 s  ep A4              77.64 s  mar G4         136.29 s  chord Dm9
                             81.43 s  chord G/F      155.26 s  mar D4
```

**Space.** Every 240–420 s the music (pads and voices) rests for 45–90 s with 8 s fades, leaving nature alone (Bernardi et al. 2006: the pause relaxes). **Fatigue guard:** after 45 min of foreground play, music fades to ambience for 10 min (06 §0.3).

### 4.6 Balance (measured, day profile)

Pad heard −20.0 LUFS-I; one e-piano note at mean velocity −21.5 LUFS-M (melody sits 1.5 LU under the pad, inside it but clearly heard); beds −21.2 LUFS-I; birds ≥ 15.8 dB under the beds. ## 5. Ambience (L0) and critters (L3)

All beds are 68 beats (17 bars) = **2 570 400 samples = 58.29 s**, stereo, OGG q4, −18.0 LUFS-I, exactly periodic (FFT-shaped noise, integer LFO cycles, filters over two periods, wrap-around grains, circular reverb). 06 asked for about 60 s: 70 beats would put the loop point 16 frames before a Vorbis packet boundary and Godot would truncate its loop fade (§10.3); 68 beats gives full room.

## 7. Haptics (D11, C3)

| Kind | Used by | Android (`View.performHapticFeedback`) | iOS `Input.vibrate_handheld(ms, amp)` (Subtle × 0.7) | Web |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 8. AudioDirector specification (for the Tech Lead)

One autoload, `AudioDirector` (`scripts/autoload/audio_director.gd`), `process_mode = PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`. It reads **only** `res://audio/manifest.json`; no level, pitch or timing is hard-coded in GDScript. `tools/audio/mixsim.py` is the executable reference of §8.4–§8.7: same data, same rules; if the game and the simulator disagree, one of them has a bug.

### 8.1 Public API (as implemented in `scripts/autoload/audio_director.gd`, ARCHITECTURE §8.3)

```gdscript

signal chord_changed(chord_name: StringName)                       # the sounding pad chord

func request_preload() -> void          # boot: threaded loads of every asset (Web: web_path)
func start_world_audio() -> void        # when the hollow becomes visible: beds 4 s, pad at 6 s, voices, birds
func play_cue(cue_id: StringName, ctx: Dictionary = {}) -> void
	# ctx: world (S01 bus), position (caption anchor), voice_step (S03/S12/S13), nap (S12),
	#      on (S15), shells (owned wind-chime shells, S21), gust (S21 on the Ambience bus), haptic (default true)
func start_purr(voice_step: int) -> void    # S03 on its dedicated player: attack fade-in, Full TICK repeat
func stop_purr() -> void                    # release fade-out, haptic repeat stops

The behaviour below is the contract; the names above are the implemented API (ARCHITECTURE §3.1, §8.3), which replaced the first draft's `caption_requested`, `start_loop`/`stop_loop` and `set_ducked`.

### 8.2 Boot

1. Parse the manifest; build `id → path` and `path → id` maps. On Web (`OS.has_feature("web")`) use `web_path` (and `banks[*].web_notes / web_variants / web_clips`) wherever present.
2. Load every stream with `ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request` while the splash shows (C8; keeps cold start ≤ 3 s). QOA stays compressed in memory (≈ 5 MB), OGG decodes on the fly.
3. Seed one `RandomNumberGenerator` with `manifest.seed` XOR the install seed; QA passes `--audio-seed=N` (user arg) to make captures reproducible.
4. Create players (§8.3), start all four beds silent, fade the profile's beds in over `music_flow.boot_bed_fade_in_s` (4 s), start the pad at `boot_pad_delay_s` (6 s) with a 6 s fade-in (GDD §3.4).

### 8.3 Player graph

| Node | Stream | Bus | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_beds[id]` × 4 | each bed's `AudioStreamOggVorbis` (beat-looped) | Ambience | always playing; gain tweened; `stop()` after 2 s at silence, restart at a seeded random position when needed |
| `_pad` | the `AudioStreamInteractive` of §4.3 (or two plain players crossfaded equal-power, §8.11 item 4; always on Web) | Music | `get_stream_playback()` → `AudioStreamPlaybackInteractive` |
| `_poly_music` | `AudioStreamPolyphonic`, polyphony 32 | Music | generative voices |
| `_poly_fx` | `AudioStreamPolyphonic`, polyphony 32 | (per call) | SFX, birds, gust chimes; `play_stream(stream, 0.0, volume_db, 1.0, AudioServer.PLAYBACK_TYPE_DEFAULT, bus)` sets the bus per voice [V] |
| `_purr` | the S03 WAV for the chosen note | SFX | a dedicated `AudioStreamPlayer` so its fades work on every platform (§9) |

All gains are tweened in **linear amplitude** with equal-power curves (in = sin(u·π/2), out = cos(u·π/2)), then written as `volume_db = linear_to_db(g)`; never tween `volume_db` linearly.

### 8.4 Conductor (pads)

- Profile → `pad_set` (day/night). Chord: uniform among the other four; hold `randi_range(6, 10)` bars (3.4286 s each), then `switch_to_clip_by_name("pad_<set>_<clip>")`; the engine quantises to the next bar and crossfades 6 beats.
- Track the sounding chord by polling `get_current_clip_index()` each frame (index into `harmony.pad_clip_order`; chord = `index % 5` into `harmony.chords`); on change set `current_chord` and emit `chord_changed`. Snapping always uses this, not the requested chord.
- A profile change that switches `pad_set` requests the same-chord clip of the new set at once (it lands on the next bar). Pad level = `profiles[p].pad_db + weather.music_add_db`, tweened over 90 s / 30 s.
- **Rest:** next rest after `randf_range(240, 420)` s; lasts `randf_range(45, 90)` s; pad fades to silence over 8 s and back; voices skip onsets from the fade start. **Fatigue:** after 2700 s of foreground play, a 600 s rest; the counter resets after it. **Swell** (S09/S10): pad +2 dB over 2 s, back over 4 s, and no voice onsets for 4 s.

### 8.5 Voices

Implement §4.4 exactly. Per active voice a repeating timer of `voices[v].period_s` started at boot (keep running across profile changes; a profile just enables/disables voices and sets base/dB). Schedule motif notes with a small time-ordered queue serviced in `_process` (± one frame of jitter is inside the ±20 ms humanisation). Look up the note file as `banks[bank].notes[str(midi)]`; it always exists because every F-Lydian note in the bank range is rendered and the motif is wrapped into `banks[bank].range`.

### 8.6 SFX and pitch rules

`snap(m, pcs)`: the nearest pitch in the set, searching offsets 0, +1, −1, +2, −2, +3, −3. `pent_step(m, k, pcs)`: from `snap(m)`, move k members of the set up or down. `wrap(m, lo, hi)`: shift by octaves into the range. Then, per rule:

| Rule | Note |
|---|---|
| walk (S01) | `n = pent_step(last, ±1)`; reflect at the bank range; first tap `snap(72)` |
| ladder (S02) | `k = 0` if > 1.5 s since the last S02, else `min(k + 1, 7)`; `n = wrap(pent_step(72, k))` |
| chord_root (S03, S05) | root pc of the current chord placed in the range (S05: G3–F4), then `pent_step(·, voice_step)` for S03, wrapped into F3–E4 |
| species (S12, S13) | `pent_step(67, voice_step)`; S12 adds a second note `snap(n ± 7)` 140 ms later (up = arrive, down = nap, nap at `nap_gain_db`) |
| chord_fifth (S08) | fifth pc of the current chord in G3–E4 |
| below_last (S11) | `wrap(snap(last_S08 − 5), D3, E4)` |
| fixed (S15, S20) | S15 on = A5 (81), off = D5 (74); S20 = A5 |
| chord_clip (S04, S09, S10, S16) | `banks[bank].clips[current chord clip]` |
| variant | uniform, never the previous variant |
| shell_run (S21) | owned shells (first N of `harmony.shell_notes`) whose pc ∈ set (all owned if none); tap = descending run, gust = 1–3 random |

Each layer plays at `cues[id].layers[i].gain_db` (plus `gust_gain_db` / `nap_gain_db` where given) plus the variant offset, on the cue's bus. Trigger SFX on **touch-down** (Android adds ≥ 46 ms of output latency, R8). After playing, call `Haptics` per `cues[id].haptic[mode]` and emit `cue_played` with `cues[id].caption`.

### 8.7 Beds, birds, critters, gusts

Target gain of each bed = `profiles[p].beds[id] + weather.bed_add_db[id]`, with `weather.replace` (rain → `amb_rain` takes `amb_stream`'s level) and crickets only if `weather.crickets`. On a phase change tween every bed to its new target over 90 s; on a weather change over 30 s. During a profile crossfade, interpolate the *targets* (equal-power), not the profiles' other fields: voices, densities and bird intervals switch at the midpoint. Birds, critters and gusts follow §5 using the profile/weather intervals; birds play via `_poly_fx` on Ambience.

### 8.8 Ducking

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| sheet opens (S07) / `NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_FOCUS_OUT` | tween the Music LPF `cutoff_hz` to `min(profile_cutoff, 1200)` in 0.4 s |
| sheet closes (S08) / focus in | tween back to the profile cutoff in 0.4 s |
| profile / weather change | tween to `max(music_lpf_min_hz, profiles[p].music_lpf_hz + weather.music_lpf_add_hz)` over 90 s / 30 s |

Get the effect with `AudioServer.get_bus_effect(AudioServer.get_bus_index(&"Music"), 0) as AudioEffectLowPassFilter`. Never touch `resonance` (§2).

### 8.9 Settings (X1, GDD §7.6)

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Music: **On** / Nature sounds only / Off | On: Music + Ambience audible. Nature only: Music bus fades out over 3 s then `set_bus_mute(Music, true)`, voices stop scheduling. Off: Music **and** Ambience fade and mute. SFX/UI are governed by the Effects slider only. The fatigue guard still applies under On. ### 8.11 Review of the current implementation (2026-10-20, against the final manifest)

**Status 2026-10-21: all six items below are implemented** in `scripts/autoload/audio_director.gd` and `haptics.gd` (equal-power `Fader` gains written with `linear_to_db`; every motif note wrapped into its bank range; `music_add_db`, `critters`, `chime_gust_s` and `pad_clip_order` read; one `AudioStreamInteractive` natively and A/B players on Web; `full_repeat_ms`, `full_delay_ms`, `once` and `haptic_once_per_burst`; variant level offsets, the 4-voice SFX cap and stopping silenced players). `tests/test_audio_director.gd` and `tools/qa/audio_smoke.gd` cover them, and the 10-minute real-play capture meets A1/A2 (docs/QA.md §3.4). The list is kept as the review record.

`audio_director.gd` already executes most of this contract (buses, beds with `replace`/`bed_add_db`, birds, pads, voices with density/gap/rest/fatigue, hush and swell, every pitch rule, Web twins, ducking, volumes, Mono, captions, haptics). Deltas, in order of audible impact:

1. **Crossfades are tweened in dB.** `_switch_pad` and `_apply_beds` tween `volume_db` from −80 to target. In dB-linear time the incoming layer stays inaudible for most of the fade and the outgoing one drops fast: at the midpoint both sit near −40 dB, so every chord change and every stream↔rain change has an audible hole. Tween a linear gain with equal-power curves and write `linear_to_db` (§8.3).
2. **Voice motifs are not wrapped into the bank range** (`_on_voice`: `snap(base ± 3)` then `pent_step`, no `wrap_into(…, banks[bank].range)`). Out-of-range notes fall back to repitching (> 1.0) natively, and on **Web, where sample voices ignore `pitch_scale`, they would sound at the wrong pitch** (§9). Wrap every motif note (mixsim does).
3. **New manifest keys** (added after the first snapshot): `weather.*.music_add_db` (rain +1 dB on pads and voices), `critters` (idle coos and night snores, §5), `weather.*.chime_gust_s` (wind-chime gust timer, §5), `harmony.pad_clip_order`, and the 8 bird variants (the first manifest collapsed them into one — fixed). Gusts can also stay with the Gameplay Engineer calling `play_cue(&"S21", {gust = true, shells = n})`; either way someone must own the timer.
4. **Pads use A/B players**, not the `AudioStreamInteractive` that D10 names. Natively that is acceptable once item 1 is fixed (the pad OGGs beat-loop seamlessly, §10.3; chord changes are timer-quantised, not bar-quantised, which is inaudible for these unmetered pads). On **Web**, hold chords 6–8 bars only so a sampled clip never reaches its end (§9). `AudioStreamInteractive` remains the D10 path and is verified (§4.3) if bar-exact changes are wanted.
5. **Haptic extras** in the cue map are not read yet: `full_repeat_ms` (S03, TICK every 120 ms while held), `full_delay_ms` (S04, TICK at +600 ms), `once` (S10), and `haptic_once_per_burst` (S02).
6. Minor: the −1.5…0 dB random level offset on variants, the global cap of 4 simultaneous SFX-bus voices, and stopping silenced bed/pad players after fades (§8.9).

### 10.3 Seamless loops — three verified recipes

1. **Content.** Every loop is exactly periodic before encoding (seam curvature 0.08–0.56; a deliberately broken loop reads 19).
2. **Vorbis + Godot ("beat-loop crossfade").** With `loop`, `bpm` and `beat_count` set, `AudioStreamPlaybackOggVorbis` loops after exactly `beat_count·60/bpm` seconds, reads the next `FADE_SIZE` = 256 frames of the file, seeks to `loop_offset`, and adds those frames with a linear fade-out on top of the restarted stream (`modules/vorbis/audio_stream_ogg_vorbis.cpp:51-123`; `FADE_SIZE` in the `.h:52`) [V]. Decoded Vorbis has independent codec error at the file's end and start, so a plain loop *steps* at the seam (measured curvature 47–140). We write `[x[0:256]·i/256, x[256:L], x[0:256]]`: the faded-in head plus Godot's faded-out tail sum to exactly x and the codec error crossfades over 5.8 ms (curvature 0.14–1.45).
3. **Vorbis packet gate.** `_mix_frames_vorbis` returns at most what is left in the current decoded packet (`:147-191`), so Godot's 256-frame read is cut short if a packet boundary falls < 256 frames after the loop point (a 70-beat bed had 16 frames). `synth.py` reads the packet table with ffprobe, rotates the periodic content until the room is ≥ 256 (two beds needed a rotation), and fails otherwise.

**Ground truth:** `verify_loops_in_godot.py` played all 21 loops (10 pads × 2 loops, 4 beds, 7 QOA purrs × 3 loops) through Godot 4.7.2 and matched the predicted signal to **3.1 × 10⁻⁵** (one LSB of the 16-bit capture) at and away from every loop point; the QOA purrs to 4.9 × 10⁻⁴ (codec), no worse at the loop points [V]. Run it after any engine upgrade: recipe 2 depends on engine internals.

### 10.4 Determinism

Seeds are `crc32(asset_id) XOR 20261020`; numpy `default_rng`; ffmpeg runs with `-fflags +bitexact -flags:a +bitexact -map_metadata -1`. Re-running on the same machine reproduces identical files (`qa/out/audio/stats.json` lists every md5). A different libvorbis build may change OGG bytes, but not the gates.
