### env

- **[major]** *02_day.png, 09, 12, 18: whole meadow from y 1100 to 2000, strongest around the sun stone and foreground creature* — The day palette sits under a milky white veil. Meadow, creatures and stone all fall into L* 70-85, the stone barely separates from the grass, and Pebblepaw's pale-blue fur almost merges with the haze. Value range is far below the §11.1 p5-p95 ≥ 55 day target, and the frame reads flat and washed out next to golden hour.
  - **Fix:** In meadow.gdshader and the L4 haze, cut the day haze/aerial mix to about 0.5× of its current value for depth 1.0 layers (haze should rise far to near, not cover the foreground). Lower day meadow L* about 6 toward the §3.1 meadow token, and add a 0.15 multiply contact shadow under the sun stone so the focal reads at a squint. Verify with the squint check that the sun-stone tile holds the max local contrast.
- **[major]** *05_night.png / 07_night_lanterns.png: lantern row x 480-980, y 1750-1900, and the brook below it* — The night focal system is under-delivered. Lantern pools are faint smudges, nowhere near radius-150px #FFC978 pools at α 0.25-0.40. Brook reflections are short, dim stubs, not vertical streaks. Glow mushrooms are the only real warm accent, so the eye path lantern → mushrooms → moon doesn't land, and the lower third reads as one dark teal mass.
  - **Fix:** Raise the lantern halo_add pool to radius 150px, α 0.35, with a smoothstep falloff that also lifts the grass albedo inside the pool (add light, not just alpha over it). In water.gdshader, mirror each lantern as a 12×120px vertical streak with sin-based ripple distortion and α 0.5. Apply a 3.3s ±5% breathing to both.
- **[minor]** *motion_report_mask.png: whole sky band y 0-1100, and 'all pixels move' across the meadow* — The sky band shows speckled per-frame change across its whole area, so grain and dither are re-rolled every frame in screen space instead of being anchored.   - **Fix:** Seed grain from world-space UV (or the object's local UV) with a time quantised to 0.5s or static, not from SCREEN_UV × TIME. Keep the triangular dither static per pixel. Re-measure motion with the grain disabled to get real layer coverage.
- **[major]** *03_golden / 10_golden_world_only: ground plane y≈1250–2000 (full res), every prop and creature contact* — The signature 'long shadows toward the viewer, fanning away from the sun' are missing. Contact shadows are short, soft blobs straight under each object, so the golden frame reads as flat ambient light with a warm grade, not a backlit sunset. The sun stone cluster has no directional shadow at all. This is the main reason #5 is below 7.
  - **Fix:** In fx/contact_shadow.gdshader, at golden and dawn, stretch the shadow quad along -g_sun_dir projected to the ground: length = clamp(2.5×object_h, 0, 220 px), with a fan angle from atan(x - sun_x). Tint the shadows with the derived shade slot (warm violet #6B5A78 at α 0.35), not black or grey. Fade the far end with smoothstep(0.6, 1.0, t) so there is a soft, lost edge.
- **[major]** *Brook ribbon y≈1580–1690 in 03, 01, 04, 10 (x 0–890)* — At golden hour and dawn the brook reads as a pale grey-lilac paved path.   - **Fix:** In world/water.gdshader, sample a vertically flipped sky gradient (sky_horizon → key) at 70 % mix, darken the 6–8 px near-bank band by 25 %, and add 2–3 scrolling, anisotropic horizontal highlight streaks (scale x8, y1) under the sun's x position. Raise glint density about 3× inside a ±150 px window around sun_x.
- **[major]** *02_day, 09_day_bloom_unfurl, 06_day_rain: whole meadow and creatures* — Day frames sit under a milky veil. ## Strengths

- Golden hour (03/10) carries the signature look: warm sun seated in the hill gap, backlit crest, peach-fuzz rim fringe on felted creatures, and tinted warm shadows. Several 540px crops work as key art.
- Coherent felted-diorama material language: soft rounded masses, fibre fringe, object grain and tone-mask props (basket, stump, mushrooms) look like one hand across creatures, flora and decor.
- Six-plane depth with clear aerial perspective at dawn, dusk and golden hour; the willow and L5 frame give convincing foreground-to-sky staging.
- UI glass and type system is clean and calm: Nunito-style rounded type stays crisp at 200%, one confident blue accent, consistent radii, and a high-opacity frosted sheet that stays out of the world's way.
- Ambient motion has at least 5 independent, gentle layers (willow, clouds, grass sway driven by wind gusts, breathing and blinks, brook sparkle) without visible pops in the strips.
- The felted creature design is excellent and consistent. Fibre fringe, blush, glossy eyes and backlit fuzz at golden (03 zoom200, frog on the sun stone) hold up at 200 % and match the best of Usagi Shima and Cats & Soup charm.
- The golden-hour palette (10_golden_world_only) is rich and restrained: violet to peach to amber sky, warm crest rim and sun halo, with no high-chroma clashes. It is the closest frame to feature key art.
- Ambient motion is layered and calm. At least 5 independent layers (willow fronds, clouds, wind-driven grass and flower sway, breathing and blinks, brook sparkle) move about 6 % of pixels per frame with no jolts, and the crest band is decorrelated.
- The night glow mushrooms at the sun stone (05 crop1) give night a focal from minute one, and the fireflies with soft halos read well.
- The UI type system and shapes are coherent and crisp: rounded display font, token radii and one confident primary blue. Vietnamese at 200 % text (19) reflows without breaking.
- The felted creature rendering is cute: fibre fringes, soft peach rims at golden hour, rosy cheeks, big low-set eyes and stubby rounded bodies satisfy the baby schema across all 12 species and both poses in the lineup.
- The overall emotional tone is calm and low-arousal. Pastel, warm-neutral palettes at every time of day, nothing harsh, no red alarms, and the dew and badge counts are small numbers. Ambient night rain (20) with the HUD hidden is very restful.
- Ambient motion is layered and gentle (V1): five or more independent layers (willow fronds, cloud drift, grass and flower sway, breathing and blinks, brook sparkle), the sky stays still apart from cloud drift, and 'z' sleep marks and expression changes add life without busyness.
- The night scene has a coherent warm/cool split: glow mushrooms at the sun stone, firefly-lantern pools mirrored in the brook, and the moon. It holds a focal from minute one.
- UI type is crisp at 200 % (rounded geometric display face, generous radii, 48 dp+ targets). The Settings comfort options (Reduce motion, Reduce transparency, Hide buttons while I watch) support the cozy, accessible intent.
