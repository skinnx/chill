## Issues by owner

### env

- **[major]** *02_day / w02_day_world, meadow and creature band y 1000-1900* — Day reads milky and flat. A pale cyan haze lies over the whole meadow and the L* p5-p95 span is only 47.7 (target ≥ 55). The sun stone is a near-white slab with no form shading, and Pebblepaw and Dandelfluff wash into it. Darks never go below mid-value except under the fg frame.
  - **Fix:** Make aerial haze depend on depth: set haze to 0 for L4 near (y > 1100) and keep it only on L2/L3. Lower the day meadow lift/ambient (meadow `#…` crest→brook) by about 6-8 L* and deepen grass-blade shade to ≈ L* 35. Give the sun stone a key/shade split (top plane L* 85, side plane L* 65 with a cool-tinted shade) so p5 drops to about L* 25.
- **[major]** *Squint test, all phases; focal box sun stone ± 200 px (540,1123 design)* — The focal never wins. The left canopy (day) and the left flower bed, stones and tufts (golden) hold higher local contrast (9.3-10.9 vs 6.9-7.8). The eye goes to the tree mass and the lantern posts, not the napping creature.
  - **Fix:** Add a local light pool on the sun stone in meadow.gdshader: a radial key boost of +8 % L inside r ≈ 220 px, which is cheap. Drop the contrast of the left canopy by lifting its shade-side cluster colour 10 % toward sky_horizon (it is mid-ground and should sit back). Desaturate and darken the left bed and stones by about 5 L*. Push the rim on the focal creature (rim 0.8 → 1.0 at golden).
- **[major]** *03_golden / w03_golden_world: lavender wash around the sun stone (crop2 top right) and vertical violet columns on the right meadow (x ≈ 840-1020, y ≈ 1300-1600)* — Golden-hour shadows and cloud shadows come out as a desaturated grey-violet overlay with readable soft-edged blob shapes. They muddy the warm signature frame, the sun stone base looks dirty purple, and the right-side columns read as artefacts rather than long shadows thrown toward the viewer.
  - **Fix:** Shift golden shadow tint from violet to warm-cool `#977B73`→`#8C7C8A` at ≤ 0.35 opacity and multiply instead of alpha-blending a lavender colour. Stretch contact/cast shadows along -g_sun_dir to the 2.5× length so they read as directional streaks. Clamp cloud-shadow noise to a low-frequency field (scale ×2) with an edge softness of ≥ 120 px.
- **[major]** *Brook glint, y 1425-1526 design, and the bottom band; motion_report brook~foreground_grass corr 0.91, about 0.09 s flicker* — The brook glint flickers at about 11 Hz. That breaks the calm 'nothing moves fast' rule and the 'no onset without a fade' rule, and it will strobe on device and in a store video.
  - **Fix:** In water.gdshader, drive glints from a 2-octave noise scrolled at ≤ 0.15 uv/s with the glint mask smoothstep(0.82, 0.95) and a per-glint lifetime envelope of ≥ 0.6 s (sin² fade in and out). Do not threshold per-frame hash noise. Cap glint brightness changes at ≤ 8 codes per frame.
- **[minor]** *06_day_rain: whole frame* — Rain is grey-green and murky rather than soft. The ground turns olive-grey, the umbrella leaves are semi-transparent outlines that read as wireframe, and the brook rings barely show. The ΔL* between creatures and meadow is fine, but the frame has no accent or light.
  - **Fix:** Make the leaf-umbrella stamp an opaque tone-mask fill using fl_leaf lit and shade, not an outline. Keep the meadow hue at the §1.4 `#97B49B` (it currently looks desaturated toward grey). Add the +15 % sky reflection sheen on the meadow, and make the brook rain rings 2× brighter (α 0.35).
- **[major]** *02_day / w02_day_world, 01_dawn, 06_day_rain: meadow band y~1050-1900 and the sun-stone cluster (540,1123 design px)* — Day reads as a milky green-cyan veil. Day L* p5-p95 is 47.7 (target >=55), and the sun stone is not the top local contrast in any phase. The left canopy and the flower beds win the squint (9.3-10.9 against 6.9-7.8). In 02_day the pastel creatures sink into the haze, and the eye has nowhere to go first, unlike the 03_golden frame.
  - **Fix:** In the day palette, lower meadow L by about 6-8 L* toward the brook and drop the L4 haze/air alpha by about 40% below y 1100. Add a soft additive key-light pool (halo_add, radius ~260 px, alpha 0.18, key colour) centred on the sun stone, and darken the stone's contact shadow to alpha 0.35, tinted toward shadow_tint. Pull the left canopy's lit/shade delta down by ~15% so the focal tile becomes the highest-contrast area. Re-run the squint test until the sun-stone tile is the top score.
- **[major]** *Brook y~1425-1526 (clip/ frames 30-212; brook and bottom band correlation 0.92, ~0.09 s period)* — The brook glint flickers at about 11 Hz. That breaks 'nothing moves fast' and will read as shimmer or aliasing on device. In stills the brook is also a flat, hard-edged lilac/teal ribbon (03_golden_crop2, 21 crop) that looks like a plastic strip, not felted water.
  - **Fix:** In water.gdshader, quantise the glint phase to <=1.5 Hz and use smoothstep fades (sparkle lifetime 0.6-1.2 s with a 300 ms fade-in) in place of the per-frame hash. Feather the bank edge 6-10 px, using a noise-modulated alpha along the shoreline, and add the felt grain/fibre fringe to the banks so they match the meadow.
- **[major]** *motion_heatmap.png: tree canopy left, stump, stones and creature outlines show doubled ghost silhouettes; region correlation 0.8-0.94 across layers* — Breeze gusts move whole masses rigidly and in lockstep, so the scene breathes as one sheet. Only 2 layers are independent before detrending. This is the main reason motion stays below the 'alive yet calm' bar and fails the R1 gate.
  - **Fix:** Drive each layer from a travelling gust front, wind_phase = t - x/gust_speed (≈180 px/s), with per-layer phase offsets: willow +0.4 s, canopy +0.7 s, beds by x. Bend vertices with height-weighted sway (amplitude ∝ uv.y²) and remove any whole-node translation on props, stumps and stones, which should be static. Keep creature breathing on its own 3-5 s period, decoupled from wind. Target: raw (not detrended) independent layers >=3.
- **[minor]** *All home stills: bottom 20% below the brook (y 1530-1920 design) and the sky band y 0-640* — Composition has a lot of dead space: an empty green floor under the brook and a large empty sky. A random 1:1 crop of the top half or bottom quarter fails as key art. Only the central band (golden crop1/2) is feature-grade.
  - **Fix:** Push the L5 foreground frame further in: fern and clover silhouettes in the bottom corners with depth-of-field blur, plus stepping stones or lily pads in the near meadow below the brook. Lower the horizon about 80 px, or add a second distant cloud bank and birds to the sky, so the image has interest in more than one band.
- **[minor]** *06_day_rain / w06* — Rain is underplayed: about 10-15 faint streaks are visible against the specified 120, no splash rings are readable on the meadow, and the brook rings are hard to see. Rain reads as overcast day with umbrellas.
  - **Fix:** Raise streak alpha to 0.30-0.45 and make sure 120 are emitted inside the visible rect (check that the emitter AABB covers y 0-1920). Add 6/s flattened splash rings at alpha 0.35, and boost the procedural ring amplitude in water.gdshader by about 2×.
- **[major]** *02_day / 03_golden / 06_day_rain: sun stone (540,1123 design px) vs left canopy (x 0-290, y 700-1000 at 900-wide display) and left flower bed* — The focal hierarchy fails the squint test in every phase. The left canopy (day) and the left bed and stones (golden) carry more local contrast (9.3-10.9) than the sun stone tile (6.9-7.8). Day L* p5-p95 is 47.7 against a target of 55 or more: the day meadow is one flat mid-green under a milky haze, so the eye drifts instead of settling. That works against soft fascination.
  - **Fix:** Day: raise the sun stone's lit face 6-8 L* (key mix +0.15 on prop_tone for spot sun_stone) and lay a 220 px radial ground pool (additive key α 0.10) under it. Reduce the canopy's internal lit/shade split about 30 % (silhouette.gdshader canopy shade mix 0.55 to 0.40). Darken the meadow at the brook edge and the near bank 5-6 L* so p5 drops and the day range reaches 55 or more.
- **[major]** *Brook y about 1425-1526 and the bottom band (motion_report: brook~foreground_grass corr 0.91, about 0.09 s period)* — A very fast shared flicker at about 11 Hz in the brook glint and bottom band reads as shimmer or strobe, not calm. It is a high-arousal cue, especially at night when the lantern streaks double in the brook.
  - **Fix:** In water.gdshader, lower the glint temporal frequency to 0.5-1.5 Hz per glint (hash-phased per cell), lifetime 0.6 s or more with smoothstep in and out, and fewer than 3 glint onsets per second on screen. Check that the foreground grass is not sampling the same time uniform at high frequency.
- **[minor]** *03_golden brook ribbon and meadow (y 1300-1650 display)* — Golden hour misses reference frame A. The meadow is olive or mustard-green (#8F8A55-ish) under a pink veil, a slightly muddy mix, instead of warm #AD8773 to #977B73. The brook reads lilac-grey instead of a bright #F5B98F ribbon, so the bottom anchor of the eye path is lost.
  - **Fix:** Shift the golden ground_front_lit and ground_back toward the bible values (hue toward 30-40°) in palettes.json tod.golden, and raise the brook's sky_horizon reflection weight to 0.7 or more with glow #F5B98F so the ribbon reaches L* 80 or more.
- **[minor]** *06_day_rain whole frame* — Rain reads as fog: streaks are almost invisible, and the scene is a uniformly washed pale grey-green with little value range. That is closer to dreary than cosy-rain. The leaf umbrellas are delightful but low in contrast.
  - **Fix:** Raise streak α to 0.35-0.45 with a slight light core, add the brook rain rings and meadow splash rings at visible α (0.3 or more), and keep the creatures' lightness at the clear-day value (ΔL* of 9 or more over the meadow) by not applying the rain chroma or lightness transform to creature fur. Darken umbrella leaves about 8 L* for silhouette.
- **[minor]** *motion: breeze gusts (raw region correlation 0.8-0.94 across canopy, meadow, brook, flowers)* — Gusts drive almost all layers in lockstep: about 7 % of pixels change every frame, and the meadow and flower beds run 17-20 %. The scene breathes as one big pulse instead of layered, independent soft motion, which is slightly busy for a resting screen.
  - **Fix:** Offset the gust phase by depth (travel the gust front at about 120 px/s from left to right so layers lag one another), cut flower-bed sway amplitude about 25 %, and give the sky clouds and willow independent slow LFOs (period 9-14 s) not tied to g_wind gust.

- **[major]** *19_night_sheet_album portrait (Pip); 05_night creature fur* — The Album portrait at night renders Pip as a dark charcoal blob on a grey card. The night ambient multiply is applied inside a UI portrait, so the collectible looks broken and unappealing at the moment players admire it.

- **[minor]** *23_touch_creature_hold1100, 21/22 tap stills, 26 lantern, 27 basket* — Touch feedback is one generic thin white ring on every target. - **[major]** *19_night_sheet_album portrait card (Pip), 13_album_friend card* — At night the Album portrait renders the creature as a dark charcoal blob with dim eyes on a grey field. It reads gloomy, even sad, and loses the baby-schema glow just when the player looks at a friend up close.
  - **Fix:** Render the Album portrait viewport with a fixed neutral-day ambient (#FFFFFF multiply, key from the day palette) independent of g_tod, or clamp the portrait's ambient multiply to a floor of L* 75. Keep the moon rim as a night accent only.
- **[minor]** *Pebblepaw at front left (about 260,1530 display) in 03_golden, 04_dusk, 16_recap* — The largest, nearest creature reads as a big dull grey-mauve mass in golden and dusk. Its cool fur under the warm ambient multiply turns muddy, and it outweighs the napping Puffmoss on the focal stone.

## Strengths

- The night frame is the best in the set: warm lantern pools along the bank, vertical streak reflections in the brook, glow mushrooms at the sun stone, fireflies and a cool moonlit sky. It delivers reference frame B and works as key art.
- Edges are crisp at 200 %: the SDF creatures have a convincing felted fibre fringe, the flower sprites are clean with no halos, and the UI text is sharp with no stair-stepping.
- The creature family is cohesive: the 12-species lineup is clearly from one hand, with consistent proportions, eye rig, blush and pastel cr_ tokens.
- Golden-hour sky gradient, the sun sitting in the hill gap, the backlit crest rim and the light shafts give the signature warm read; the lantern posts and brook sparkles carry the eye path downward.
- The UI glass, dock and sheets are calm and high-opacity with token radii and one confident blue. They layer cleanly over the world, and at 200 % text the Vietnamese layout survives.
- The golden-hour signature frame (03_golden, w03): warm sun in the hill gap, light shafts, backlit crest and peach-fuzz fibre rims on the creatures at 200% zoom. The centre 1:1 crop is genuinely key-art grade and stands next to Usagi Shima and Cats & Soup.
- The felted creature rig: 12 species with distinct accessories and silhouettes (20_lineup), clean fibre fringe edges, readable blinks and sleep states, and a charming, consistent face language.
- Night lantern system (05, 26): warm pools on the far bank with vertical streak reflections in the brook, glow mushrooms at the sun stone and fireflies give a clear night focal plan.
- Edge and type quality: crisp text at 200% (28 zoom), no stair-steps or halos on creature or prop outlines, and banding passes everywhere.
- Draw calls at 88 of 100 with garden_full, plus deterministic, clean gates (186/186 GUT, strict check clean).
- Creature design is irresistibly cute: the baby schema is strong (large low-set eyes with catchlights, blush, round felted bodies, tiny limbs). Closed-eye nap poses and 'z' glyphs read instantly as safe and restful. The lineup shows 12 distinct, harmonious species.
- Night with lanterns is the emotional high point: warm lantern pools, vertical reflections in the brook, glowing mushrooms at the sun stone, sparse fireflies and stars. It is calm, low-arousal and genuinely soothing. Preserve it.
- Edge quality and the felt look: the fibre fringe on creatures, the golden peach-fuzz rim visible at 200 %, and crisp, well-oversampled UI text. Everything looks like it came from one hand.
- The UI is calm and uncluttered: a frosted dock, large targets, generous padding, and friendly copy ('While you were away', 'Say hello'). Vietnamese at 200 % text still lays out cleanly.
- Ambient life after detrending has 4 independent layers (flower beds, willow and chimes, horizon shafts, clouds), plus blinks, friend-facing turns, butterflies and the dusk-to-night palette transitions. It feels alive without demands.
